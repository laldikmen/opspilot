## What the scores leave out

Of 72,070 orders in these months, 243 are not scored at handover, and they hold 186 of the 5,460 late orders. They were handed over after their promised day, or have no handover record, or were delivered first, so a late order among them needs no model: the missed promise is already visible.

Flagged orders at handover have a median of 18.5 days left before the promised date: that is the time an operations team has to act.
